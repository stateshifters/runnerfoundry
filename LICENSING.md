## Public license

The AGPL permits anyone to use, copy, modify, and redistribute RunnerFoundry. It also permits charging for RunnerFoundry,
support, hosting, and related services. Recipients keep the rights granted by the AGPL for every version they receive under it.
Buying separate commercial terms does not remove those public rights.

Redistributed binaries must be accompanied by the corresponding source in one of the forms permitted by the AGPL. Each official
binary release and Debian package identifies the exact release source. GitHub release source archives provide the corresponding
source for official builds.

If a modified version supports remote interaction over a computer network, AGPL section 13 requires its operator to offer the
corresponding source to those remote users. RunnerFoundry does not currently expose a direct user-facing network interface.
Whether indirect interaction through another service triggers section 13 depends on the actual deployment and modification.
Operators are responsible for evaluating their compliance obligations.

The AGPL does not require a downstream developer to open a pull request. It also does not require disclosure of separate and
independent services merely because they communicate with RunnerFoundry. Whether combined software forms one covered work is a
fact-specific legal question.

## Contributing improvements

RunnerFoundry prefers improvements to be contributed upstream. A shared implementation reduces duplicated maintenance, makes
fixes available to everyone, and gives operators a clearer upgrade path. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) before opening a
pull request. By submitting a contribution, a contributor accepts the inbound license grant in that guide. The grant keeps the
contribution in the public AGPL edition while allowing the project copyright holder to offer the combined work under alternative
commercial terms. It requires no separate signature or automated agreement check.

## Commercial licensing and custom work

Stateshifter Labs Limited offers alternative commercial licensing, custom engineering, integrations, and support by request.
Start a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/stateshifters/runnerfoundry/issues) with a non-confidential summary and ask the
maintainer to establish a private contact channel. Do not include confidential, personal, credential, or security-sensitive
information in an issue.

Commercial terms provide an alternative to AGPL obligations when agreed in writing. They are not required for ordinary
commercial use that complies with the AGPL.
