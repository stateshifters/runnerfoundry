# RunnerFoundry Trademark Policy

The RunnerFoundry name and any official logos identify the project and releases published by its maintainers. The software
license grants copyright and patent permissions. It does not grant trademark rights.

## Uses that do not require permission

You may use the RunnerFoundry name to:

- Refer accurately to the project.
- State that your software or service uses, supports, or is compatible with RunnerFoundry.
- Link to the official project or documentation.
- Identify an unmodified official RunnerFoundry release.
- Use the voluntary "Powered by RunnerFoundry" acknowledgement.

These uses must not imply endorsement, sponsorship, certification, or official project status.

## Forks and modified distributions

Forks and modified distributions are welcome under the AGPL. A modified distribution must use a name that clearly distinguishes
it from the official RunnerFoundry project. It may describe itself as "based on RunnerFoundry" or "RunnerFoundry-compatible" but
must not present itself as an official release.

Do not use official logos or branding as the primary identity of a modified distribution without written permission. Preserve
copyright and license notices as required by the software license.

## Commercial services

Commercial services may accurately state that they are powered by or use RunnerFoundry. The service name and presentation must
make clear who operates it and must not suggest that the RunnerFoundry maintainers operate, certify, or endorse the service.

For uses outside this policy, open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/meza/RunnerFoundry/issues) with a non-confidential summary
and request a private contact channel.
